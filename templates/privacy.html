Privacy Policy

Your privacy and security are our top priorities at CityCare Hospital

Introduction

At CityCare Hospital, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal and medical information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our hospital services and website.

Information We Collect

CityCare Hospital may collect the following information:

  • Patient name, age, gender, and contact details
  • Medical history and health records
  • Appointment details
  • Billing and payment information
  • Insurance details
  • Account login information

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used for:

  • Providing medical and healthcare services
  • Managing doctor appointments
  • Maintaining patient records
  • Processing billing and insurance claims
  • Improving hospital facilities and services
  • Communicating important health updates

Data Security

CityCare Hospital follows strict security practices to protect patient information from unauthorized access, misuse, modification, or disclosure.

Sharing of Information

CityCare Hospital does not sell or misuse patient information. Information may only be shared with:

  • Doctors and healthcare professionals involved in treatment
  • Insurance companies for claim processing
  • Government authorities when required by law

Cookies Policy

Our website may use cookies to improve user experience, remember preferences, and analyze website performance.

Patient Rights

Patients have the right to:

  • Access their personal medical information
  • Request correction of incorrect details
  • Ask questions about privacy practices
  • Understand how their information is used

Changes to This Policy

CityCare Hospital may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact CityCare Hospital.
